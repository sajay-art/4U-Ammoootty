GOOD TO KNOW

FAQ

Can I paste a YouTube or Instagram post link?

The site identifies popular social URLs but cannot extract arbitrary posts. Use official download/export options for your own or authorized content, then upload the file for conversion.

What direct links work?

Public links that point directly to audio/video files, do not require login, and permit downloading. Some servers block automated requests.

What are the limits?

Direct URL downloads are limited to 100 MB. Uploads are limited to 200 MB. Render free instances can have memory, time and bandwidth limits.
