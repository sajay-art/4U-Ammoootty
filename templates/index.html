GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked.

Is 4U Ammoootty free?

Yes. This starter project has no accounts or paid tiers. Hosting providers may impose usage limits.

Can I download any YouTube or Instagram link?

No. This version does not extract media from social-platform URLs. Use platform-provided download features or upload files you have permission to process.

Are my files stored?

Uploaded source files are removed after processing, and generated output is removed after it is sent. Avoid uploading sensitive files to a public server.

What is the maximum file size?

The default upload limit is 200 MB. Your hosting provider may impose a lower request or execution limit.
