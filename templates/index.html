HELP CENTER

Frequently asked.

Which websites are supported?

The backend uses yt-dlp, which supports many public websites. Support can change when websites update their systems. Not every link is guaranteed to work.

Why might a link fail?

A website may require login, block automated access, restrict downloads, or change its delivery format. Private, age-restricted and DRM-protected content is not supported by this hosted service.

Is the service always online?

Free hosting may sleep when inactive and has limited CPU, memory, disk and bandwidth. Large or lengthy downloads may fail or be interrupted.
